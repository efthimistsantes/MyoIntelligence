About the app

MyoIntelligence is a reference for human muscle anatomy and visual representations of movement. It includes anatomical illustrations, animations and learning quizzes.

Educational use only. The images and animated movements are anatomical illustrations, not instructions or encouragement to perform exercises. This app is not designed for self-assessment, for teaching movements to others or for diagnosis, treatment or rehabilitation. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before attempting any movement or exercise.
