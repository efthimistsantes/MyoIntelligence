Privacy Policy

How MyoIntelligence handles locally saved preferences and requests for online anatomy images.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Who operates this app

MyoIntelligence is an educational iOS application about human muscle anatomy and anatomical movement. It is provided by [DEVELOPER_NAME_OR_COMPANY] (the “Developer”). Privacy enquiries: [SUPPORT_EMAIL].

2. Information stored on your device

The app may save your selected language, quiz progress and related learning preferences locally on your device using Apple’s local storage (including UserDefaults). If a saved-items feature is made available, related preferences may also be stored locally.

The app does not require account creation. In the version reviewed for this policy, the Developer does not operate an account database or an app server that receives these locally saved records.

3. Internet connection and external content

To display muscle images and anatomical animations, the app downloads content from Wikimedia Commons. These connections necessarily send technical network information, which can include your IP address, device/network request details and the time of the request, to the remote service and network intermediaries. The Developer does not receive your local quiz progress through these image requests.

Wikimedia processes information under its own privacy policy. Following links to image attribution, licensing or other external websites may also involve separate third-party data practices.

4. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The reviewed app code does not include a third-party advertising SDK, an integrated analytics SDK or user account sign-in. The Developer does not sell user data or use this app to track users across other companies’ apps and websites.

Third-party services that host requested external content may collect technical data under their own policies, as described above.

5. Data retention and your choices

Locally saved preferences and quiz data stay on your device until you reset them using an available in-app control or remove the app. Removing the app normally deletes its local app data from the device; data in Apple device backups is managed according to your Apple backup settings.

The Developer cannot directly delete records held independently by Wikimedia or your network provider. For those requests, consult the applicable third-party privacy policy.

6. Contact and privacy rights

To ask about this policy or exercise applicable privacy rights (including requests for access, correction or deletion where relevant), contact [SUPPORT_EMAIL]. If you email the Developer voluntarily, your message and contact address will be used to address your enquiry and retained only for as long as reasonably needed for that purpose or to meet legal obligations.

Since the app’s learning preferences are stored locally rather than in a Developer account, you can manage them on your own device.

7. Privacy of this website

This public policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It contains no Developer-operated analytics scripts or advertising trackers. GitHub, as hosting provider, may process technical request information when you visit the site. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the most recent revision. Please review this page for the current policy.
